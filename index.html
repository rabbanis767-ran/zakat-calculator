⚖️ Nisab Type

Nisab is the minimum amount of wealth a Muslim must own for Zakat to become obligatory. It is calculated based on the total value of your eligible assets including cash, bank balance, gold, and silver.

Silver Nisab: Value of 612.36 grams of silver
Gold Nisab: Value of 87.48 grams of gold

Silver Nisab is commonly recommended as it lowers the threshold and allows more people in need to benefit.
